Privacy Notice

Last reviewed: 7 November 2026

This notice explains how Property Kickstart collects and uses personal information when you use our websites, services, forms, publications, property services and digital tools, including services operated under Property with Hanna.

1. Who we are

Property Kickstart is responsible for deciding why and how personal information covered by this notice is used.

For privacy enquiries or to exercise your data-protection rights, contact privacy@PropertyKickstart.co.uk.

2. Personal information we use

The information we use depends on how you interact with us. It may include:

  • your name and contact details;
  • information you provide when making an enquiry, completing a form, subscribing to communications or corresponding with us;
  • information about a property and the circumstances relating to it;
  • photographs or documents you choose to provide;
  • subscription, consent, unsubscribe and objection records;
  • information about property owners or other relevant individuals obtained from another person or from publicly accessible sources where necessary for a property-related purpose;
  • information needed to provide a requested digital service or property tool;
  • information you provide through investor surveys, including investment experience, priorities, preferences and challenges, together with relevant submission records; and
  • limited technical and security information generated when you use our online services.

We do not intentionally ask people reporting a property to provide unnecessary sensitive personal information about other individuals.

3. Why we use personal information

We may use personal information to:

  • provide and operate our services;
  • respond to enquiries and requests;
  • assess property-related information and opportunities;
  • investigate or verify information provided to us about a property;
  • identify or contact a property owner where appropriate and lawful;
  • manage subscriptions and communications;
  • maintain records of consent, objections and unsubscribe requests;
  • conduct investor research, understand investor priorities and improve our services;
  • protect, administer and improve our services; and
  • comply with legal obligations and establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

4. Our lawful bases

Depending on the circumstances, we rely on one or more of the following lawful bases:

  • Consent — for activities where the law requires consent, including relevant marketing communications.
  • Contract or steps before a contract — where processing is necessary to provide something you have requested or to take steps at your request before entering into an agreement.
  • Legitimate interests — where we have a legitimate business purpose and your interests, rights and freedoms do not override that purpose. This may include responding to genuine enquiries, assessing and verifying property-related information, operating and protecting our services, and considering appropriate property opportunities.
  • Legal obligation — where we must process information to comply with the law.
Your right to object. Where we rely on legitimate interests, you have the right to object to the processing in relevant circumstances. Contact privacy@PropertyKickstart.co.uk to raise an objection.

5. Information about empty or potentially empty homes

We may receive information about a property from its owner or from another person who knows or believes that the property may be empty.

Information received from a third party is treated as information to assess and is not treated as proof that a property is empty.

Where necessary, we may combine information provided to us with information available from lawful public sources to assess the position, verify relevant facts, identify an owner or decide whether further contact is appropriate.

If we obtain personal information about you from someone else or from a public source, the information may include your identity or contact details, your connection with a property, property ownership information and other property-related information relevant to the purpose for which it is being used.

The source may be a person who submitted information to us or a lawful publicly accessible source.

Where the law requires us to provide privacy information to someone whose personal information we obtained indirectly, we will do so within the applicable legal timeframe unless an exemption applies.

Information reported to us about a property is not made publicly searchable through our reporting service.

6. Our five engagement and data journeys

We operate five distinct engagement journeys across Property with Hanna and ReportAnEmptyHome.co.uk. Each has a different purpose. Giving us an email address or other personal information for one journey does not automatically enrol you into another.

Journey 1: Saturday Supplement subscription

If you choose to subscribe to the Saturday Supplement, we use your contact details and subscription records to manage the newsletter relationship, send the welcome communication and future editions, and record your consent, unsubscribe and suppression choices.

This is a marketing subscription. Where consent is required, we ask for affirmative marketing consent and may use a double opt-in process. Requesting a resource, reporting a property or responding to us about a property does not by itself subscribe you to the Saturday Supplement.

Journey 2: Empty Homes resource request

If you request the Empty Homes resource through the /options journey, we use the information you provide to deliver the requested resource and administer the specific resource follow-up journey.

A resource request is separate from the Saturday Supplement subscription. If the same form offers an optional Saturday Supplement checkbox, that choice is separate and unticked by default. We only start the Saturday Supplement subscription process if you actively choose that option.

Journey 3: Empty-home reporter contact

If you report an empty or potentially empty property and choose to provide contact details, we use those details for operational and service purposes connected with the report, such as acknowledgement, clarification or follow-up where needed.

The property report, property information and supporting evidence are managed separately from the minimum contact information used for service communications. Providing reporter contact details does not constitute marketing consent and does not automatically add you to a newsletter or promotional nurture journey.

Journey 4: Reported-property owner response

If you identify yourself as the owner of a property that has been reported, respond to contact about that property, or request an owner-specific resource, we may use your contact details and relevant property or case information to respond, provide requested information, administer the owner journey and continue appropriate service communications.

Responding as a property owner does not itself amount to marketing consent. Where an owner-specific guide, nurture sequence or other ongoing marketing communication requires consent or another specific permission, that permission is captured separately.

Journey 5: Investor Survey

If you complete a Property Kickstart investor survey, we collect your answers, the survey edition or year, a submission reference and the date and time of submission.

You may participate without providing your name, email address or mobile number.

We use survey responses to understand investor priorities, identify common challenges, conduct research and improve our services.

Where you voluntarily provide an email address, we may associate your survey responses with an existing investor profile or assessment, including one created subsequently.

Survey participation does not automatically subscribe you to marketing. Where marketing email consent is offered, it is a separate, optional choice.

If you request access to our WhatsApp community, we provide a joining link. Selecting this option does not automatically enrol you.

We do not publish individually identifiable survey responses.

Common controls across the five journeys

For each journey, we may record information such as your email address, name where provided, the page or form you used, the source brand or campaign, submission date and time, relevant resource or property reference, consent status, consent wording or version, unsubscribe or objection status, and technical source information needed to operate and evidence the journey.

One email address may be associated with more than one journey where you have genuinely entered those journeys separately. We do not treat participation in one journey as permission for another.

You may unsubscribe from marketing or withdraw marketing consent at any time using the unsubscribe facility provided or by contacting us. Service communications that are still necessary for a separate property or resource request may continue where there is another lawful reason to send them. We may retain the minimum information necessary to ensure that an unsubscribe request or objection continues to be respected.

7. Where information is required

Where we mark information as required in a form, we need that information to provide the relevant service, respond to the request or complete the relevant process. If required information is not provided, we may be unable to proceed.

Information not marked as required is optional unless we tell you otherwise.

8. Children and young people

Some of our property-reporting services may be used by children and young people. We aim to collect only the information reasonably needed to receive and assess a report and to administer any reward that later becomes payable.

Where a feature is restricted to adults for safeguarding reasons, we may ask you to confirm that you are aged 18 or over before giving access to that feature. Where a simple age confirmation is sufficient, we do not need your full date of birth and will not ask for it merely for that purpose.

Children and young people can have the same data-protection rights as adults. Where appropriate, we provide privacy information in clear, age-appropriate language and take account of the best interests of children when designing our services.

If additional consent, parental involvement, identity verification or another safeguard is required by law in a particular situation, we will explain this at the relevant point rather than applying it unnecessarily to every child or young person.

9. Who we share personal information with

Where necessary for the purposes described in this notice, personal information may be shared with or processed by appropriate categories of recipients, including:

  • organisations that provide technology, hosting, communications or administrative services on our behalf;
  • professional advisers and other service providers where needed for a legitimate business or legal purpose;
  • public authorities, regulators, courts or law-enforcement bodies where disclosure is required or permitted by law; and
  • other parties where disclosure is lawfully necessary in connection with a transaction, legal obligation or legal claim.

We do not sell personal information as a data product.

We do not disclose information about an owner simply because another person has reported a property.

10. International transfers

If personal information is transferred outside the UK, we use an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism or safeguard where UK data-protection law requires one.

11. How long we keep personal information

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it is being used and for any applicable legal, regulatory, accounting or claims requirements.

The period will depend on the type of information and the reason we hold it. In particular:

  • enquiry and property-related information is retained while it is genuinely needed to assess, respond to or manage the matter and for an appropriate period afterwards where necessary;
  • information that is no longer required for an active or lawful purpose is deleted or anonymised where appropriate;
  • photographs and supporting evidence are not retained longer than the purpose for which they are needed;
  • subscription information is retained while the subscription is active;
  • investor survey responses are retained only while needed for research, service improvement or legitimate investor relationship purposes; information no longer required is deleted or anonymised as appropriate, and maintaining annual survey editions does not mean retaining identifiable responses indefinitely; and
  • limited suppression or objection records may be retained for as long as needed to ensure that a person's choice continues to be honoured.

12. Cookies, pixels and similar technologies

Our online services may use technical functions that are necessary for them to operate.

We may also use non-essential cookies, pixels or similar technologies for analytics, advertising, audience measurement or to understand the effectiveness of our communications and campaigns.

Where the law requires consent for these technologies, we will provide the required information and obtain consent before they are activated. You will be able to refuse or withdraw that consent.

If advertising or social-media tracking technologies are used, they may process information about your device, browser and interactions with our websites. We will not use them to disclose the content of private property reports, uploaded evidence or other sensitive submission information to advertising platforms.

13. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights including:

  • the right to access your personal information;
  • the right to correct inaccurate information;
  • the right to request deletion;
  • the right to restrict processing;
  • the right to object to processing;
  • the right to data portability where applicable; and
  • the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

To exercise a right, contact privacy@PropertyKickstart.co.uk. We may need enough information to identify the relevant records and verify your identity where appropriate.

14. Automated decision-making

We do not use solely automated decision-making that produces legal effects or similarly significant effects on individuals through the services covered by this notice unless we specifically tell affected individuals otherwise before such processing begins.

15. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

16. Changes to this notice

We review this notice when our services, legal obligations or uses of personal information materially change.

Where the law requires updated privacy information to be brought to the attention of affected individuals, we will do so at the appropriate time.
